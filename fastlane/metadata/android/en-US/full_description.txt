* Sehat (صحت, meaning health in Urdu) is a privacy-focused personal health tracker for Android. Track your steps, heart rate, and meditation while keeping your data on your device.

**Pedometer**

* Count steps using your device's built-in step counter sensor
* Track steps, distance, calories, and active duration
* Set and track a daily step goal
* Adjust sensor sensitivity: Low, Medium, High, or Very High
* View individual session history and details
* Calculate BMI using your personal information

**Heart Rate Monitor**

* Measure heart rate using your device's camera
* Place your finger over the camera to measure your pulse
* View real-time BPM with a live chart
* Configure measurement duration and sensitivity
* Record measurements with activity context:
  * Walking
  * Running
  * Cycling
  * Resting
* View previous heart rate measurements

**Meditation**

* Relax with built-in ambient soundscapes
* Six soundscapes included:
  * Rain
  * Water Waves
  * Lightning
  * Bird Chirping
  * Wind Breeze
  * Forest
* Add your own sounds from device storage
* Choose 5, 10, 15, 20 minutes, or a custom duration
* Continue playback using a foreground service
* Display rotating motivational quotes

**Data Backup**

* Export your health data as a JSON file
* Import your data whenever needed
* Choose the backup location using Android's Storage Access Framework
* No storage permission required

**Privacy**

* All health data is stored locally on your device
* No account or login required
* No cloud sync or servers
* No advertisements
* No analytics or crash reporting
* No `INTERNET` permission
* Camera frames used for heart rate measurement are not recorded or stored
* Health data leaves your device only when you explicitly export a backup

**Permissions**

* `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION` — Count steps
* `CAMERA` — Measure heart rate
* `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` — Show foreground service notifications
* `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` — Background step tracking and meditation playback
* `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` — Select custom meditation sounds on Android 13+
